About The Project

Architectural Excellence in West Hyderabad's Growth Corridor

Godrej Neopolis Hyderabad represents a flagship high-rise residential development positioned near Neopolis Circle in West Hyderabad. Built across a 5-acre land parcel, the project integrates modern vertical architecture with approximately 80% open landscaped spaces designed to maximize natural light, privacy, and cross-ventilation.

Offering expansive 3 BHK (2,350 sq. ft.), 3.5 BHK (2,500 sq. ft.), and 4 BHK (3,350 sq. ft.) floor plan configurations, each residence is crafted with entrance foyers, wide balconies, continuous living and dining areas, and dedicated utility zones. With direct access to the Outer Ring Road (~500 metres) and close proximity to Financial District (4.4 km), Gachibowli (6.7 km), and Kokapet, Godrej Neopolis combines executive urban connectivity with a peaceful residential sanctuary.

*Figures regarding 456 residences and 3B+G+48 floors reflect available third-party project site information and are subject to official RERA clearance and developer confirmation.
